The project combines mathematical method development with real, challenging biological data — the goal is not merely to apply existing PDE/ML tools, but to develop new combinations.

CTU PI: Jooyoung Hahn (CTU Prague; computational modelling). MIND-MAP partners: Paweł Dłotko (University of Warsaw; Topological Data Analysis) and Sandra Siegert (ISTA, Austria; microglia biology and imaging).

What you get

  • Funded PhD position (≈ 43,000 CZK net a month) within the international MIND-MAP project
  • A research problem at the intersection of geometry-informed Topological Data Analysis, PDEs and deep learning in real scientific data
  • Close collaboration with researchers in Warsaw and at ISTA
  • Access to unique 3D and 3D+time microscopy datasets
  • Opportunities for conferences and research visits

Who we are looking for

  • PhD candidates: a master's degree in mathematics, applied mathematics, computer science, physics or a related quantitative field
  • Strong background in numerical analysis and interest in topology, geometry or data analysis
  • Readiness to program — Python and/or C++
  • Good English, and curiosity, dedication, willingness, good teamwork and positivity about interdisciplinary research
